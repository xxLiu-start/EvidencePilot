# Claude API data retention and Zero Data Retention

- **Resource ID:** 10
- **Publisher:** Anthropic Claude Platform
- **Official source:** https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/api-and-data-retention
- **Purpose:** Review retention, ZDR eligibility, feature exceptions and third-party boundaries.

## Reading notes

The guide explains retention behavior across APIs and features and the scope of Zero Data Retention. ZDR is organization-level and does not cover every model, feature or product surface. Verify the exact API, model, tool and cloud route with legal/security teams.

## Enterprise selection questions

- Does the data contain personal or confidential information
- Are the selected model and features ZDR-eligible
- What do tools and logs retain
- Are DPA and regional requirements satisfied?
