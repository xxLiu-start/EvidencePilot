# Model Studio dedicated deployment overview

- **Resource ID:** 04
- **Publisher:** Alibaba Cloud Model Studio
- **Official source:** https://help.aliyun.com/zh/model-studio/model-deployment-introduction
- **Purpose:** Compare token-based billing with dedicated compute deployment.

## Reading notes

The guide distinguishes token-usage billing from dedicated model-unit/compute deployment and describes deployment and billing choices. Dedicated capacity may suit stable high-concurrency or low-latency workloads. Confirm current pricing and supported models in the console.

## Enterprise selection questions

- Is traffic stable enough to reserve capacity
- What are SLA, concurrency and latency targets
- Is resource isolation required
- How will idle capacity be controlled?
