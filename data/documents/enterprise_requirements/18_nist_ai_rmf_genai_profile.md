# NIST AI RMF Generative AI Profile (AI 600-1)

- **Resource ID:** 18
- **Publisher:** NIST
- **Official source:** https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence
- **Purpose:** Establish risk identification, assessment, governance and monitoring.

## Reading notes

NIST AI 600-1 is a Generative AI profile companion to the AI Risk Management Framework. It helps organizations address trustworthiness risks across design, development, use and evaluation. It is a governance framework, not a model leaderboard.

## Enterprise selection questions

- What risks arise in this organization's use case
- Are pre-launch evaluation, red teaming and monitoring in place
- Are owners and incident processes defined
- Are models re-assessed after changes?
