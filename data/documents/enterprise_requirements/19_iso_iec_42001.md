# ISO/IEC 42001:2023 AI management system

- **Resource ID:** 19
- **Publisher:** International Organization for Standardization
- **Official source:** https://www.iso.org/standard/42001
- **Purpose:** Understand requirements for an organizational AI management system.

## Reading notes

ISO/IEC 42001 specifies requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining and continually improving an AI Management System. Use it to review governance roles, risk management, supplier management and lifecycle controls. The full standard may require purchase.

## Enterprise selection questions

- Is there an AI management system and accountable owner
- Are third-party model providers governed
- Are model/data/use-case changes controlled
- Are risk and approval records retained?
